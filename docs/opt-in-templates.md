```

Out of every closer I've worked with, you're on the short list. Hand-picked. Not random.

Real homeowners. Real bills. Real shot.

Here's the play:
— 7AM each day I text you. Reply YES to draw, NO to skip, PAUSE to step out. Reply GO when you're back.
— Fresh lead lands → CALL and TEXT within 5 min → reply CONTACTED {first_name}.
— Miss the clock? Lead goes to the next rep. No drama. No penalty for pausing — only for sitting on it.
— Top 3 each day eat first the next morning. That's how you build the line.

Heads up: this is BETA. Lead quality, volume, and timing will vary. Some days it's a fire hose, some days a trickle. We're tuning the engine in real time and your feedback shapes it.

But the reps who treat every lead like the only one they're getting? Those are the ones I'm building this around. That's you. That's why you got tapped.

Reply READY to lock in.

Let's go.
— [YourName]
```

## Contest announcement (when you fire up a daily / weekly prize round)

```
🏆 [BRAND] CONTEST — LIVE NOW.

$[N] cash to whoever stacks the most points by [time] ET tonight.

POINT SYSTEM:
• CALL = 2 pts
• TEXT = 1 pt
• SET = 5 pts × however many leads you've worked today (engagement multiplier — the more leads you work, the bigger every set pays)

HOW IT RUNS:
• You get one lead at a time
• After each lead, reply with what you did:
  — CALLED [first name]
  — TEXTED [first name]
  — SET [first name]
  — MISS (releases it, no penalty if you're moving fast)
• Reply NEXT when you want the next lead. No NEXT = no new lead lands.

Stay sharp. Move fast. The rep who treats every lead like the only one they're getting wins this.

Reply READY to lock in. Game on.

— [YourName]
```
